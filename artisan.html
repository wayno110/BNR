Distinctive Designs, Consistent Performance

Sanctum

The Sanctum combines deep ebony tones, elegant ivory-style points, and shimmering abalone panels that shift between blue, green, violet, and gold under changing light. Every angle reveals a different play of colour, creating a cue that is as captivating to admire as it is to play with.

Obsidian

Designed around a deep obsidian-black aesthetic accented with premium burl wood and crisp white diamond inlays, this cue delivers an elegant yet commanding presence at the table. The clean point work, geometric ring design, and hand-finished details create a cue that stands out without being flashy.

Eclipse

Inspired by the dramatic contrast of light and shadow during an eclipse, this cue features striking ivory-style spear points set against deep black and exotic reddish wood veneers, complemented by shimmering abalone inlays that reveal different colours under changing light.
